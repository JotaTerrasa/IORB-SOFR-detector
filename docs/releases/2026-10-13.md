# Liquidity dashboard v2 — 2026-10-13

## Scope

Rebuild of the existing public dashboard, retaining the core SOFR/IORB/BTC analysis, exploratory direction hypothesis, simulation, risk calculator, alerts, local journal and CSV export. Adds separately dated TGA/reserves/ON RRP context. Moves unvalidated trading instructions out of the overview into an explicitly experimental laboratory. Same public site and repository; no authentication or data-sharing changes.

## Corrections

- API responses previously persisted indefinitely in the cache-first service worker. APIs now bypass it completely; shell is network-first. Browser copies are explicitly marked old after source failure.
- Historical rate gaps no longer use today's rate. Same-day SOFR/IORB comparison; actual SOFR observations for correlation, without duplicated weekends.
- Genuine daily Coinbase spot USD OHLC replaces CoinGecko multi-day candles and invented close-only candles. Excludes unfinished UTC day; timestamped price uses the same spot USD source.
- Publication calendar is reflected in simulation and SOFR freshness, including US securities holidays. Next-open entry, ATR from real OHLC, stop-before-target convention, commissions and unfinished-position exclusion.
- Risk calculator validates LONG/SHORT price geometry, includes commissions inside the budget, warns on insufficient margin or liquidation before stop.
- Legacy local journal migrated without deleting v1 records. No account balances or journal entries sent to a server.
- Local Chart.js with its license; CDN/fonts removed; tighter CSP, bounded proxies, upstream timeouts, bounded per-instance rate-limit memory and no caching of upstream errors.

## Limits

The laboratory is exploratory and not validated out of sample. Simulation uses the current revision of historical rates and habitual publication dates rather than a point-in-time vintage archive. Funding and intraday slippage excluded. Liquidation is approximate. Macro series have different frequencies and are not combined into an exact liquidity predictor. Per-IP limits are per serverless instance. A daily or weekly data source does not become live simply because the page refreshes often.
